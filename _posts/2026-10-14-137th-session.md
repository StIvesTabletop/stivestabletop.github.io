It was shaping up to be an epic game, think Donald Byrne vs Bobby Fischer, or Björn Borg vs John McEnroe. At the last minute Simon and Iker were left to play [Food Chain Magnate][FCM] as a two-player game (sadly Jyo had to drop out). This is Splotter's 1950's themed US fast food game about marketting and selling more than your opponent(s). It's also a playful commentary on capitalism. For those who love a bit og bling, there is a super fancy version of the game... but the basic edition is grand.

You win by having he most money the second time the bank breaks. Each round you have employees in your corporate structure, each employee lets you do something -- hire new employees, train them, produce pizzas or burgers, make drinks, advertise to the masses, change prices, manage more employees, open new restaurants, or build new homes (making more demand). It's all a delicate dance of supply and demand, trying to undercut the competition, and all the while keeping your own company moving.

Simon has played many times before and, perhaps a tad cruelly, proceeded to demonstrate all the mistakes Iker made: mis-timing a price war, inefficient placement of a new restaurant, devestating use of foliage (adding gardens to houses makes them pay more), and showing the power of marketing.

This is a tricky game, it takes a couple of plays to see the moves and counter-moves. The margins are thin. In a two player game a mistake can mean you earn nothing in a round. Splotters are not to everyone's taste (it's a drink pun!). Nice to see this at the club for the first time, and it is a highly recommended game (do not play five player with new players... it will be very messy).
