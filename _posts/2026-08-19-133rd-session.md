title: 133rd session - Clankety Clank!
---

One long game was so popular it was played on 2 tables by half of the gamers, noisily venturing into the dragons lair to steal treasures whilst the others built space bases and inventions.

Short starter games for this session were [My City][MyC] now on its third consecutive week and [Money][Mon] a trading up and set collecting game back from [session 131][131].

![My City: Roll And Build](/images/posts/2026_08_19/MyCity01.jpg "My City: Roll And Build"){:class="img_post"}

The [My City: Roll And Build][MyC] table managed the first three scenarios before it was time to break out the long games. The first timers struggled a bit in the first round and were caught out with some rocks and empty spaces generating negative scores but they rapidly learned from this and better optimised their pieces in the next rounds. Some good co-localisation of the same building types started splitting the scores more. So the final scores over the 3 rounds were close enough that no one felt they had really failed their town planning but a couple of players had done enough in rounds 2 and 3 to earn themselves some city building credentials.

Then we managed to work out enough pieces from a single combined Clank! box (to reduce the number of boxes to carry!) to play both original [Clank!: A Deck-building Adventure][C] and [Clank!: Catacombs][CC] with a few expansion set parts thrown into both.

Classic [Clank!][C] with one of the starter game maps but with [Adventuring Party characters][CAP] Monkeybot prime, Garagnir the orc and D'Allan the eleven collector, to give some asymmetry, and some cards mostly from the [Egyptian themed expansion][CMC] thrown in. It is a deck building and push your luck game, head down into the dungeon depths, grab as much treasure as you can carry and get out before the dragon damages you too much. However not too many dragon cards were drawn so this game did not have quite the sense of peril as usual made it a gentle introduction for the two new players. It did hamper monkeybot (chosen by teacher Kathy) as he was not being damaged and so not gaining extra permanent actions.

![Clank!](/images/posts/2026_08_19/Clank01.jpg "Clank!"){:class="img_post"}

They got the hang of the mechanics after a few rounds and the dash to the depths for the best artifacts commenced. James was doing well acquiring cash and then lots of shop items and starting to trigger his elven collectors bonuses for good effect. Richard was not being too violent with the orc whose abilities trigger by murdering the monsters lurking in the dungeons, but was playing a fairly balanced game and acquiring a good variety of the treasures and cards with end game victory points. Monkeybot was stomping around making a lot of noise and taking the best artifact but only got hurt by the dragon twice when they decided the dungeon was being cleaned out and it was time to get back to the surface.

James emerged only one turn after Kathy and Richard and before the dragons rage really started triggering due to the end game mechanic that causes dragon bag cube "Clank!" draws when a finished player would have had their turn. Scores were fairly close with Richard on 112, Kathy on 120 and James "the collector" on 143.

Iain, Neil and teacher Jeremy were at the same time delving into [Clank!: Catacombs][CC] with the [Lairs and Lost Chambers expansion][CCLL] and with the other adventurers from [Clank!: Adventuring Party][CAP] to spice things up - Neil choosing Whiskers the cat, Iain venturing forth with Agnet the Dwarf and Jeremy was helped by Lenara the wizard. 

![Clank! Catacombs](/images/posts/2026_08_19/ClankCatacombs01.jpg "Clank! Catacombs"){:class="img_post"}

This is very much a similar game to the original - build your deck to improve your skill, might and speed, and then use these to explore the catacombs and find treasure and defeat it's denizens whilst avoiding the ghost dragon by not making too much noise (Clank!). The differences to original [Clank!][C] are exploration tiles - so the map is not known at the start and lock picks to navigate the locked tunnels and claim treasures or rescue prisoners. There are also ghost rooms that haunt all the players (by inflicting damage), portals to skip around the map, and waypoints that can help accumulate gold the more you visit.

Whiskers zoomed off at the start and quickly brought the rage of the dragon upon the others, a naughty side effect of the cats powers. Agnet bravely set forth and was doing a good job of building a skilful deck that allowed her to net several secret tomes over the course of play. Lenara had chosen some rather noisy skill cards that was attracting the dragons ire, but not finding much booty, though a lucky flute helped her purchase a shiny crown from the shop.

Whiskers was the first to net an artifact and enrage the dragon further, and with all the Clank! being made by all three of us, the pain being dealt out was quite even, but harsh. So much so, that Agnet decided with the tomes, a monkey idol and an empty backpack that was enough for today and headed for the exit. Lenara was desperately trying to find an artifact, but they kept disappearing (denizens on the dungeon row kept returning them to the dragon's hoard), until luck shined and one stayed long enough for a grab and run. 

![Clank! Catacombs](/images/posts/2026_08_19/ClankCatacombs02.jpg "Clank! Catacombs"){:class="img_post"}

Whiskers was scampering through the rooms looking for a way to pick up a monkey idol to go with a skill card they had picked up. Agnet exited the dungeon which now caused more dragon attacks, hoping that the others would be caught. Luckily for both Lenara and Whiskers the dragon didn't seem to want to play with them, so they got off lightly even though they had both managed to go to the shop to buy a healing necklace in anticipation. Lenara teleported to the exit next, but Whiskers wasn't too far behind managing to loot a monkey idol and zip through the dragon's legs.

In the end, Agnet's plan of making the others take the brunt of the dragon's wrath didn't work to her advantage, with her haul being a tad less than the others at 43, though Lenara's 68 just pipped Whiskers' 65 to the top spot. A fun, yet more chaotic game than the original as less planning can be done, but enjoyable none the less.
