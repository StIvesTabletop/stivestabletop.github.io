title: 132nd session - Worms or Wyrms?
---

More adventuring this session, through history, fantasy and space with longer games featuring Dragons, Sandworms, Vikings and miniature fantasy nations.

A club classic [Kingdomino][King] (now played at 18 sessions) was the first short game out with 4 players getting 2 games in. New player Jemima had a good first game, dominating the scores with her extensive mines, but Emma just pipped her in the second game with some big scores in a couple of terrain types.

![My City - Roll and Build](/images/posts/2026_08_05/MyCity01.jpg "My City - Roll and Build"){:class="img_post"}

[My City - Roll and Build][MyC] was [back][131] for another go, this time a starter game with 6 people. We played the first two episodes of chapter 1 which just introduces the dice and the different buildings. James, Jeremy, and Natasha all got the same score of 16 on the first scenario, but the second one proved more tricksey for everyone and so the scores rather differed. Richard just pipped James by 2 points and took first place with 41 points (for a combined episode 1 and 2 score).

Whilst four of the players departed for other games, James and Jeremy (whilst waiting for Simon to arrive) decided to play the last episode of chapter 1 which introduces the well. Whilst a fun time of building buildings was had, James was the clear winner this time, as he pushed his luck at the end rolling the building dice and getting 2 single buildings helping him to easily fill in his map and come top.

Following a session at the pub last Wednesday new players Ian and Dan expressed a desire to play [Dune: Imperium Uprising][DIU] again. Unfortunately, Dan couldn’t make it to the Corn Exchange but Dave, Iain, Tasha and Richard sat down to play instead. Tasha and Richard were new to the game so there was a bit of a teach required prior to starting. The game flows very well but there are a lot of symbols and board spaces to explain, plus a few perhaps overly fiddly rules involving maker hooks, shield walls and sand worms.

![Dune: Imperium Uprising](/images/posts/2026_08_05/DuneImperiumUprising01.jpg "Dune Imperium Uprising"){:class="img_post"}

Richard and Tasha picked the game up quickly and Richard (focusing on fulfilling contracts) started to move up the score board with Dave and Tasha in pursuit. Dave purely by accident ended up with vast quantities of spice and so was able to buy huge armies to compete in the end of round battles. Unfortunately he was a little overcautious and tried not to over-commit troops meaning that he was pipped a couple of times by other players using combat cards. The game was also a little unusual as the card market got clogged up early with very high cost cards meaning that players were unable to improve their decks as the starting cards don’t offer much in terms of buying power.

In the end all players agreed to scrap the market cards and deal out new ones which helped move the game along. There is an optional expansion that gives players a single atomics token that they can use once per game to clear the market. This will be included from now on. Sometimes the game can run a little long and as a result they were the last table to finish. In the end Richard took the win on 11 points, with Dave and Tasha tied on 8 and Iain bringing up the rear on 5.

![Wyrmspan](/images/posts/2026_08_05/Wyrmspan01.jpg "Wyrmspan"){:class="img_post"}

[Wyrmspan][WS] was selected again for its sixth club outing. Three players were soon excavating, enticing and exploring the caverns and collecting dragons with varying abilities. The rounds focused on shy, then large, 0 or 1 egg slot and finally 4 point or higher dragons, these scores didn't really separate the players out much but Jemima was just in the lead.

![Wyrmspan](/images/posts/2026_08_05/Wyrmspan02.jpg "Wyrmspan"){:class="img_post"}

In the end it was dragon specialities that made the difference with Emma doing well from end of game abilities and eggs, Jemima from high scoring dragons and Kathy just taking the victory helped by 20 points from tucked cards.

After Wrymspan concluded and all the other long games were still in progress, the girls decided to head off down the tavern to discuss their recent adventuring exploits in [Braggart][Brag]. A simple card driven boasting game to end the night, now on its 17th club play. Players need a minimum of two cards (deed and foe) to tell a story but can add scene and result cards for additional flavour. Then the others can challenge their extraordinary tales with liar cards and swap in less impressive activities. So an epic battle with the mother of all dragons can be reduced to wetting yourself in front of Johan the friendly trader. After numerous trips for all to the bar (to refill with more story cards) it was Emma who had the best run of tall tales to become adventuring champion (or best liar).

Next session is on Wednesday August 19th 7.30pm at the St Ives Corn Exchange. Pop in to the [Discord][Contact] servers a day or so before and see what games are being discussed and book a seat if one tickles your fancy. Or just turn up on the night and go with the flow, there are always lots of games to suit all tastes or a chance to try something new.
