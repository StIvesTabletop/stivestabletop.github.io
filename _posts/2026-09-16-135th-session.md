title: 135th session - Busted
---

A wide variety of games were selected this session, including time travelling bus trips, and dice rolling alien survival and secret role games.

The early arrivals got tucked in to [Patchwork Doodle][PD] - a simple game of tessellation but with an emphasis on creating the largest squares! Its a quick roll and write game where you have to attach 6 polymino shapes to your individual starting patch per round. You then score for your largest square. Repeat for 2 more rounds and add up to find the best patchworker. There are a few special abilities you can use over the course of the game to help you out, but best quilter wins.

The next players to arrive tried ever popular and simple to learn [Kingdomino][King] now on its 19th club session. Kathy taught 3 new arrivals this simple domino based tableau builder, which was so popular they played it again. 

Then the longer games started with the pre-advertised on Discord [Project: ELITE][PE] - Dave

Then 3D tile game [Planet][Plan] was chosen. After a quick explanation the 4 players were adding pentomino tiles to their magnetic planet core trying to work towards their secret terrain based goal and entice animals to their planet. Which animal recruits would be available each round was known from the start of the game so some planning ahead could be attempted. Animals have particular terrain based needs, either the largest terrain touching or not touching another terrain type or the most fragmented sections of a terrain type. Each round 1 tile is selected and added to the core and then everyone compares their growing planet to the animals requirements. The first animal, a blue whale, that liked lots of ocean areas was hotly contested, it took three rounds to break the deadlock as a draw means the animal continues into the next round. In the end animal enticement based scores won out over the secret terrain goal scores.

Leaving the final table trying [Bus][Bus] - Simon

Then with plenty of time to spare after the planet building that table moved onto temple building with [Cleopatra][CatSoA]. This is a fairly simple game, on your turn you either visit the market to acquire building materials or you visit the quarry and add some of the 6 types of architecture (column walls, doors, pedestal and throne, mosaic, sphinxes and obelisks) to Cleopatra's latest temple. The twist is that some of the materials are a bit dodgy and Cleopatra will notice, so you need to find ways to reduce your corruption by incorporating sanctuaries for Anubis into the mosaic or giving the highest offering to the Priests in a hidden bid. Once 5 of the 6 building types are completed the game ends and the corruption tokens hidden in each players pyramid of shame are compared and the player that cut the most corners is thrown to the crocodiles.

In this game it was teacher Kathy who failed to shed enough corruption by end game who was busted by Cleopatra and became a repast for the reptiles, while Alex had managed to stash the most cash to take the win.

The other tables then teamed for a few round of [BANG! The Dice Game][BANG]. This was a full game of 8 players, where you have 1 sheriff and 2 deputies, 3 outlaws and 2 renegades. Its a deduction game as you only know who the sheriff is at the start of the game. The sheriff (and deputies) win if the outlaws are eliminated, but the outlaws win if the Sheriff dies. A renegade wins, only if they are the last standing! Everyone has a different character with an ability that helps you break one of the rules of the game. Each turn a player gets to roll the dice up to 3 times, where the dice heal (by drinking beer), shoot the players next to you, cause arrow attacks by indians, blow you up with dynamite, or allow you to shoot everyone with a gatling gun.

Richie explained the rules and then proceeded to tell everyone about a time where he got killed on the very first turn and nearly flipped the table in annoyance. So of course the first round of the game had several people pick on Richie, though luckily didn't quite finish him off. It was all quite cagey with pot shots going off everywhere, apart from a few people still being picked on by others! Then a win for the outlaws as the Sheriff was struck down by one too many arrows, just before deputy Simon had figured out the outlaws and was going to pull a win for the good guys.

Next session is on Wednesday September 30th 7.30pm at the St Ives Corn Exchange. Do join us to see what games will be on the tables, cooperative manic dice rolling or more sedate but crunchy competitive games?
